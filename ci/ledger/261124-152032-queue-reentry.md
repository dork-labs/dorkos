When the merge queue takes a PR out (a failed group build, a conflict with an entry ahead, `main` red),
GitHub turns its auto-merge off. Since #4200 nothing turns it back on: PRs get auto-merge when they
open and nobody arms, and agents may not re-arm other agents' PRs. So an ejected PR waits for someone to
notice. On 11-24 #4233 and #4216 sat green and mergeable from about 12:30 to after 15:00.

The change: `.github/workflows/queue-reentry.yml` runs `scripts/queue-reentry.sh sweep` from main's
copy, with the `dorkos-merge-tail` app. A PR qualifies when its latest merge-queue event is a removal by
the queue itself (never a person's or a workflow's dequeue), its auto-merge is off and nobody turned it on
or off since. If it is green on every check in `ci/required-checks.json` and mergeable, auto-merge goes
back on, pinned to the head it judged (`expectedHeadOid`). If it conflicts with `main`, the author gets
one comment per head. A hold, a failing or pending check, a head already put back twice, and a head
`queue-repeat-guard.sh` would take straight back out are left alone. It never goes red.

It runs when `test` or `pr-gates` finishes (on a merge group, when ejections happen, or green on a PR)
and every 10 minutes as a backstop, because scheduled runs here are throttled: review-redispatch.yml ran
once in the 15 hours after it landed.

Target: 0.3, below the arming-era share, since conflicts still wait for their author. Report p50 minutes
from ejection to re-entry beside it (target under 30) and `tracked.repeat-ejections`, which must not
rise. Revert, or narrow it to failed-checks ejections, if repeat ejections rise or a re-entered head is
ejected again for the same reason on more than a third of re-entries: then putting PRs back costs queue
builds instead of saving idle time.
