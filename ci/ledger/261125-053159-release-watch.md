Design §6 "Watch" (DOR-5123): with one release a day and no approval click, a bad release must be seen
by a machine, not by whoever next installs it. `release-watch.yml` runs once when Release Publish
succeeds, then on the after-push CLI Smoke Test tick (cron backstop) at most hourly while the newest npm
release is under 24 hours old. It checks `npx dorkos@X.Y.Z --version` on Linux, macOS and Windows,
`npm view` for the three packages, the GHCR image's `/api/health`, the Homebrew formula and both
`/download/*` links, and lists new issues naming the version. A failing group opens or updates one
`release-watch` issue per version and closes it once the group passes. Docker, Homebrew and the
desktop links count as skips while they are only behind, until they ship daily
(`RELEASE_WATCH_SURFACES: daily`) and for 180 minutes after the publish.

Target: every broken surface of a release has its issue within 60 minutes of the publish (the first
watch runs at the publish, then hourly). Evaluate after 14 days of daily releases, against the
releases published and the issues opened.

Revert or retune if it opens issues for releases that work (false alarms on two releases in a row),
opens more than one issue per version and group, or misses a break that people report first.
