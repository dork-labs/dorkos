`open-pr-cap.sh` (DOR-3341) now refuses a new PR at 30 open, non-draft PRs into
`main` instead of 10: Dorian's call on 2026-11-15. The fixture in
`test-open-pr-cap.sh` pins the new default (29 open passes, 30 refuses), and the
`creating-pull-requests` skill says 30.

The script is advice an agent runs before `gh pr create`; no workflow, required
check, retry, shard or timeout reads it, so no metric in `ci/metrics.yaml` can
move. `DORKOS_OPEN_PR_CAP` still overrides it and `--p0` is unchanged. Revert if
the review line backs up again at the higher cap.
