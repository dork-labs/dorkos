dorkos-cloud #463: under `set -o pipefail`, `cmd | grep -q x` can read a match
as a miss, because grep exits at its first match and the writer dies of
SIGPIPE (76 in 5,000 on a loaded runner). Two workflow steps here run under
pipefail with that shape:

- `test.yml`, "Pick the shard count": a miss on a pull request touching
  apps/server or packages/shared picked two shards instead of six. Every test
  still ran, but such a sweep can reach the 30-minute ceiling on two (ledger
  261031-150702). Both greps now read a here-string.
- `update-homebrew.yml`, "Update desktop cask": a miss skipped the cask update
  with the DMG on the release. That grep now reads to EOF
  (`| grep -x … >/dev/null`).

The pattern, the flags and the branch each outcome takes are unchanged. The
repo-wide rewrite and a lint that keeps the shape out land after the Nov 17
cut. No required check, deadline, shard count, retry or coverage rule changes.
