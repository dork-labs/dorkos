DOR-5123 (daily release, design §5): once nobody clicks approve on `npm-release`, the `gate` job
decides right before npm whether release vX.Y.Z, cut from main commit P, may ship.
`scripts/release-gate.sh` is that decision, as `facts` (read-only GitHub reads) and `decide`
(prints `SHIP` or `REFUSE <reason>`). It refuses unless P is on main's first-parent history with no
revert and no open auto-revert PR, the newest completed full run of `browser-test` on P (a `push`
or `schedule` on main) succeeded, and every context in main's `ci/required-checks.json` passed in
P's own merge-queue build. Any read it cannot make is a refusal. The release tree is not checked
again: `proof.ts` in the `prove` job already proves the tip, the cut point and the release commit's
paths.

Verified Nov 24 on 5b7c44b15e: a squash queue merge lands the queue's own commit on main, so the
merge_group check runs sit on P's SHA, in suites whose head branch is `gh-readonly-queue/main/*`.
A commit merged around the queue (633a8e5ed9, Nov 24) has none and is refused.

The script and fixtures landed first. The `gate` job in `release-publish.yml` now runs it after
`prove` and `prepare`, with P taken from `prove`'s output (set only once `proof.ts` has shown P is
the branch's merge-base with main), and `publish` needs `gate`, so a REFUSE stops the publish with
the reason in the job summary and an error annotation. Only routine mode is gated (hotfix stays
refused). `FULL_RUN_WORKFLOWS` is `browser-test.yml` until the full unit sweep runs on main
(PR #4257). The approval click stays on until go-live;
`scripts/__tests__/release-publish-gate.test.ts` pins the job graph.

Baseline (Nov 24): nothing re-checks P at publish time; a person's approval is the only gate.
Target: with the gate wired, no release ships from a P that was reverted, red in its full run, or
not merged green through the queue, and no green P is refused for a gate bug.
Evaluate: after the first 3 daily releases with the gate wired, against each run's facts and decision.

Revert if it ever ships a P that fails one of its checks, or refuses a green P twice running.
