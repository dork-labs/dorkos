# Your sidebar

The column down the left of DorkOS is a list of places: the channels you talk in, the people and
agents you message, and the pages you go to most. It never lists single chats or threads. Those
have pages of their own, so the sidebar stays short and stays put.

From top to bottom:

1. **Your workspace's name** with a menu, and the **New** button.
2. **A search box**, which opens the same search as ⌘K.
3. **Threads** and **Directory**, two pages that are always there.
4. **Your own sections**, when you have made any.
5. **Channels**.
6. **Direct messages**, ending with a row to message someone new.
7. **Add-ons**, the pages your mini apps (the app calls them extensions) add, when you have any.

Nothing in the list moves on its own. Channels and direct messages sort the way you choose, and
your sections hold what you put in them, in the order you put it there.

## Every heading folds

Click a heading to hide what is under it. Hold `Alt` (or `Option` on a Mac) while you click and
the whole panel folds at once.

A folded heading keeps its unread rows in view underneath it, so folding something never hides
a message you have not read.

## Threads and Directory

**Threads** opens a page with every thread you are in, across every channel and direct message,
newest reply first. Each one shows the room it is in, the message it started from, how many
replies there are, and the two newest. You can reply right there, or click the room's name or
the first message to open the thread in its room.

**Directory** opens your team page, with every agent and person you can reach.

## Direct messages

Your messages with people, your group messages, and the agents you talk to one on one.

An agent appears here once you have a direct message with it, or once you pin it. Click it and
you are in your chat with that agent, the same one every time. An agent shows up once in the
sidebar, never twice. Your other agents are in the Directory, and ⌘K finds any of them by name.

The last row, **Message an agent or person**, opens the Directory so you can pick someone. Before
you have any direct messages it reads **Message an agent** and opens straight onto your agents.

A group message stays a group message. Pick two or more people or agents from **New › Group
message** and it gets its own row, named after whoever is in it, unless you named it yourself.

## What you build: sections

**Sections** are the groups you make. A section can hold anything you see in the sidebar: a
channel, a direct message, an agent, or a mix. Your sections sit just below Threads and
Directory, so the thing you organised is the thing you see first. Make one from **New › Section**,
or right-click any row and pick **New section**.

Some sections fill themselves from a rule you set, like "agents active right now". The rest you
fill by hand, by dragging rows into them, and they keep the order you put things in.

**Channels** and **Direct messages** are listed by name, and each one remembers if you would
rather sort it by what happened most recently. That choice lives in the heading's `⋮` menu.

Threads, Directory and Add-ons are pages, not lists you arrange. You cannot drag a row into
them. If you try, DorkOS says so and tells you where to drop it instead.

## Add-ons: your mini apps' pages

A mini app can add a page of its own. **Add-ons** lists those pages at the bottom of the sidebar,
each with its own name and icon. Click one to open it. The page you are on is marked, so you can
see where you are.

There is no Add-ons heading until a mini app adds a page. It stays under a space's channels when
you open a space. If two pages share a name, each row also says which mini app it belongs to.
⌘K finds the same pages by name.

## Where everything else lives

The sidebar lists places, so a few things you might look for live somewhere else:

| Looking for…                                     | Where it is                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| A chat with an agent you had earlier             | ⌘K, or the agent's profile, which lists its chats         |
| A thread you were part of                        | The **Threads** page, at the top of the sidebar           |
| Something waiting on you, like an approval       | The Inbox in the top-right corner, and Home on your phone |
| A scheduled run, or work an agent did on its own | **Schedules** and **Activity**                            |
| An agent you have not messaged yet               | **Directory**                                             |

<Callout type="info">
  ⌘K finds chats, channels, agents and pages by name. To search inside your messages, press ⌘⇧F.
</Callout>

## The card at the bottom

One card sits just above the row of buttons at the bottom, and only ever one. Whatever matters
most gets the spot: finishing setup, then an update that is ready, then a question about your
work, then a tip.

Every card has an × and hiding one sticks. Your answer is saved to your account rather than to
the browser you happened to be using, so a card you hide on your laptop stays hidden on your
phone.

## On a phone

Below tablet width there is no sidebar and no slide-out menu. There are three tabs along the
bottom instead:

- **Home** is the whole sidebar list. Anything waiting on you, like an approval, sits at the top
  as a card you can answer right there. The number on the tab counts those, and nothing else.
- **DorkBot** opens a chat with the built-in agent that knows how DorkOS works.
- **You** holds your account and the rest of the app.

Switching tabs keeps your place in each one. Nothing has to be swiped away before you can use
what is underneath.

A few things work differently under a thumb:

- **Press and hold any row** for the same list of actions a right-click gives you on a computer.
  A press that turns into a scroll never opens it.
- **A thread opens over its room**, filling the screen, with the message it started from pinned
  at the top. Tap back or swipe right to close it, and you are where you were in the room.
- The card at the bottom of the sidebar appears at the bottom of Home instead.
