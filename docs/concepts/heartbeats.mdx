# Heartbeats

An agent with a job should not wait for you to ask. A heartbeat lets it wake up on its own, look at
what changed, and do the next useful thing inside its job. Most of what it does shows up as finished
work and one line in the record, not as messages you have to read.

Each time an agent wakes up is called a **beat**. In the app, a beat shows up as a **check**.

## How an agent wakes up

An agent wakes in two ways:

- **On a timer.** About every 30 minutes during working hours, and every 2 hours outside them. It
  checks more often while something it promised is due today, and less often when nothing has
  changed for a day.
- **When something happens.** Someone on your team names it in a room and it could not answer, a
  promise it made comes due, one of its scheduled tasks fails, one of its own chats fails, or one of
  its connections stops working.

Messages from people outside your team (someone on a bridged Telegram or Slack chat, for example)
never wake an agent. Several things happening within a minute become one beat, not five.

Each agent's `HEARTBEAT.md` file decides which of these it uses. You can open it on the agent's
**Heartbeat** page. The setting at the top, `wake`, takes one of three values:

| `wake`   | What happens                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `timed`  | It wakes on the timer and when something happens. |
| `events` | It wakes only when something happens.             |
| `off`    | It never wakes on its own.                        |

An agent with no `HEARTBEAT.md` wakes only when something happens. Agents that run on your own
Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode plan start that way too, because a beat every 30 minutes would spend
that plan.

## What a beat does

Most beats cost nothing. DorkOS first looks at what changed without running any AI model at all. If
nothing changed, the beat ends there and leaves one line in the record, like "Checked 4 things.
Nothing new."

When something did change, a cheap check decides whether the agent needs to look. Only then does the
agent itself run, in that day's heartbeat chat. That chat is a normal chat you can open and type in.

Inside a beat the agent picks one of four moves for each thing it found: act, act and then tell
someone, ask, or stay quiet. It acts on what is inside its job. It asks only when the call belongs to
someone else. A beat where it says nothing at all is a good beat.

## How it tells you

When an agent has something to say, it picks the quietest way that still works:

1. A line in the record.
2. A line in its next report.
3. A post in a room.
4. A direct message to you.
5. A notification outside the app.

DorkOS then handles the manners for it:

- **Your hours.** A message to you waits until your working hours start, unless it is urgent. Set
  your hours and time zone with **Your hours** on your own profile.
- **Urgent means you must act now.** Money or data still at risk, a security problem still open, a
  customer being harmed, or a deadline that will be missed without you. A problem the agent already
  fixed is not urgent. It goes in the next report.
- **One message, not five.** Several messages from one agent that are due at the same time arrive as
  one.
- **Said once.** An agent does not raise the same thing twice. If it tries, the repeat goes into its
  next report instead.
- **No duplicates.** When two agents raise the same thing, you get it once.
- **Away.** When you mark yourself away, routine messages wait for your return. Urgent ones still
  arrive.

You can see what is waiting for your hours on each agent's **Heartbeat** page.

## Reports

Every agent reports to someone: you, or another agent. Set it with **Reports to** on the agent's
profile. Leave it blank and the agent reports to whoever created it.

Once a day by default, at the start of its manager's working day, the agent writes one short report:
what it finished, what is in progress, what is at risk, and what it needs. When nothing happened
worth your time, it skips the report. You can change the rhythm to `weekly` or `off` with `report` in
its `HEARTBEAT.md`.

An agent's promises live on its **Commitments** page, where anyone on the team can read them. When a
promise comes due, the agent wakes up to deal with it.

## Cheap checks on DorkOS credits

When your computer is linked to a DorkOS account, the agent's **Heartbeat** page asks once: **Run
cheap checks on DorkOS credits?** A cheap check is a small, fast model that decides whether a change
needs the agent at all. It lets most beats end without a full run.

<Callout type="info">
  Nothing is spent on credits until you choose **Use credits**. Choose **Not now** and nothing
  changes.
</Callout>

Until you say yes, a free rule-based check still filters out the obvious, and anything else runs on
the agent's own model. The page always says where checks run right now, for example "Checks run on
DorkOS credits. 41 today."

## Is it helping?

The **Measures** section of the **Heartbeat** page shows whether an agent's work on its own helped
over the last 7 days, and what it cost your attention. Every number comes from the record, never from
the agent's own account of itself.

| Measure                              | What it counts                                                                        | Starting target |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Work that stuck**                  | Work it did on a beat that nobody undid within 7 days                                 | 95% or more     |
| **Useful messages**                  | Its messages to people that got a reply, or a reaction other than 👎, within 48 hours | 60% or more     |
| **Interruptions per useful outcome** | Messages that reached a person, divided by work that stuck plus useful messages       | 1 or less       |
| **Surprises**                        | Not measured yet                                                                      | 0               |
| **Mutes and pauses**                 | Times a person paused it, switched its heartbeat off, or muted it                     | Trending to 0   |

Today, work counts as undone when a person stopped that beat's chat, paused the agent within an hour
of it, or reacted 👎 to what it sent. DorkOS does not see reverted code yet. A target is a starting
point to compare against, not a grade. Under **Supporting measures** you can also see how many beats
were quiet, how many promises were kept on time, and what the cheap checks used.

Agents can read these measures too, for themselves or for any other agent.

## Stopping a heartbeat

- **One agent:** set `wake: off` in its `HEARTBEAT.md`, or pause the agent. A paused agent does not
  beat until you resume it.
- **Every agent:** run `dorkos config set heartbeats.enabled false`. Messages already waiting are
  kept, and go out when you turn heartbeats back on.

## Reference: HEARTBEAT.md

This part is for people who want to edit the file by hand. The settings sit at the top, between two
`---` lines. Everything below them is plain notes for the agent: what to watch, how to judge, what
never to do without asking, and the shape of its report.

```markdown
---
name: heartbeat
description: What Juno watches between conversations.
wake: timed
report: daily
---

- Invoices: anything unpaid past 14 days gets the second reminder.
- Never touch pricing; ask Dorian.
- Report shape: finished, in progress, at risk, needed. One line each.
```

| Setting  | Values                                                                 | When left out                 |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `wake`   | `timed`, `events` or `off`                                             | `events`                      |
| `watch`  | Any of `addressed`, `commitments`, `schedules`, `health`, `workspace`  | Everything except `workspace` |
| `report` | `daily`, `weekly` or `off`                                             | `daily`                       |
| `sweep`  | How often, at most, to take a full look around in working hours (`4h`) | Never                         |

Edits apply on the agent's next beat. If the settings at the top stop making sense, the last good
ones stay in force and the **Heartbeat** page says what is wrong.
