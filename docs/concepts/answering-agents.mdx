# Answer your agents from anywhere

Sometimes an agent stops and waits for you. Maybe it wants to edit a file and needs your OK
first. Maybe it's asking you a question it can't answer on its own, like which test runner to
use. DorkOS calls this an **Ask**.

You don't have to be looking at that agent's chat to see it. An Ask shows up everywhere in
DorkOS at once, so you can answer it from wherever you happen to be.

## What an Ask looks like

An Ask always says what the agent actually wants, in plain words:

> **Meeting Notes wants to edit standup.md**

Not "waiting on you." Not a vague notice. You always know what you're being asked to allow
before you allow it.

## Where you'll see it

The same Ask shows up in four places at once:

- **The header**, on every page. A small badge counts how many things are waiting on you.
- **The sidebar**, under Heads up.
- **The home screen**, at the top, if anything needs you.
- **The channel or session it came from**, on the line right above where you type. If an agent
  is asking you something in a room, that room's line turns to a question you can answer on the
  spot.

Open any one of them and you're looking at the same card. Answer it from any of the four, and it
disappears from all the others in the same moment.

## Answering it

Click **Allow** or **Deny**. That's it.

If you'd rather use the keyboard: press `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Y` to jump straight to the next thing
waiting on you. Once the card has your focus, `A` allows it and `D` denies it.

If an agent asks several similar things at once, like reading five files, they arrive as one
card instead of five. Answer once and you're done with all of them.

## When nobody answers

An Ask counts down for ten minutes. That part has not changed: the card shows the time left, and
turns amber and then red as it runs out.

What happens after those ten minutes is that your agent waits. The countdown is replaced by the
words "waiting for you", and the card stays exactly as answerable as it was in the first minute.
Go to lunch, sit through a meeting, do the school run: the agent is still holding the question
when you get back, and answering it picks up where you left off.

Four hours later, the agent gives up. The request is refused, the agent carries on without it,
and it tells you how long it waited. Nothing is lost when that happens. It just means the agent
didn't get to do that one thing.

A scheduled task is the one exception. Nobody is sitting there watching it, so there is nobody to
wait for: its questions are refused after ten minutes and the run carries on.

Once an Ask is answered, one way or another, it leaves behind a short record: what you chose,
or that it was already answered and when, or that time ran out. You can always find that record
later, in the same place the question first appeared.

## Answering in a shared room

The question itself is never posted to the channel. Everyone in the room sees only a much
shorter notice, something like:

> Meeting Notes is waiting for you to approve something before it can carry on.

No file name. No command. No countdown. The full question, with the file it wants to touch and
the buttons to answer it, goes to your DorkOS cockpit and nowhere else.

<Callout title="What DorkOS doesn't do yet">
  An Ask only reaches you while your DorkOS cockpit is open. Nothing is pushed to your phone or sent
  as a notification when the cockpit is closed, so a question raised while you are away waits
  quietly until you come back and look. And DorkOS today answers to one person, so there's no way
  yet to say who else may approve things, or for which agents.
</Callout>
