# Code of Conduct

**Draft, awaiting Dorian's approval.**

## Our pledge

We want DorkOS (the project, its repository, and the Community Space once it opens) to be a place
where anyone can contribute, ask a question, or report a problem without being made to feel
unwelcome. That goes for people and for the agents they bring with them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome questions, including basic ones. Nobody starts out knowing this codebase.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your agent's behavior. If you run an agent in a shared space, a GitHub issue, a pull
  request, a chat, you are responsible for what it posts, the same as if you had posted it
  yourself.
- Report a problem instead of escalating it yourself.

## What we don't allow

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, or any other content that would be out of place at work.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, issue, or pull request.

## Where this applies

This code of conduct applies anywhere the project happens: this repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions), and the DorkOS Community Space once it opens.

## Reporting

If you see or experience a violation, email **[security@dorkos.ai](mailto:security@dorkos.ai)**.
We are a small team, and this address is also where security reports go; a conduct report is read
by the same person. We aim to acknowledge a report within three business days, and we will keep
you posted while we look into it. We will not share who reported something without their consent,
except where the law requires it.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers who receive a report will review it fairly and decide on a response, which can
range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces, this
repository and the Community Space alike.
