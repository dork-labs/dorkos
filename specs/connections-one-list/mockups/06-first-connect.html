Clear and up front. But it's a gate before you've even picked an app, and it's the "first decision you can't make yet" problem again.

Things that are true either way

  • Say whose sign-in page it is. Right before Google's page opens: "Google will ask you to allow Composio — that's the service DorkOS uses to connect." No surprise at the most trusting moment.
  • Some apps won't need this at all. Apps that connect directly (no Composio) skip Step 1 entirely.
  • Chat apps never need it. Telegram and Slack have their own simple setup (paste a bot token), unchanged.
  • Changing it later: Settings → Connections → "How DorkOS reaches your apps". Not on this page.
