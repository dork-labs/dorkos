The four rules

  1. They're rows, like everything else. A small "Chat" tag is the only difference. No separate Messaging section.
  2. They skip the one-time setup. Connect goes straight to the app's own steps. So on a brand-new install, Telegram works in two minutes with nothing else set up.
  3. One row per app, even when it does two things. Slack asks "talk to agents" or "let agents use it". Everything else just connects.
  4. Plumbing stays hidden. The internal "Claude Code · internal · Serving 35 agents" row disappears — it's how DorkOS works inside, not something you connect. Webhook goes under "For developers". Chat apps added from the Marketplace show up as rows too.
