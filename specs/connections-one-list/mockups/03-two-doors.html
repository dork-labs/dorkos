Why "Try it" buttons, not "a prompt to copy"

A prompt you copy and paste is homework. A button that opens the chat with the message already in it is one click. It also teaches what Gmail is for. If you picked no agents in step 3, the button still works: you pick an agent, and it walks you through Door 1.
