How the pieces fit

  • One app (just Gmail): side panel › More › Disconnect. Tells you which agents lose it.
  • The whole setup (every app that goes through it): Settings › Connections › Disconnect. Lists every app that will stop working, and the button says the number.
  • Switching (Composio key → DorkOS account): add the new way, then reconnect apps one by one. Rows connected the old way keep working until you do. We never move logins behind your back.
  • Nothing set up yet: this section just says "Set up when you connect your first app" with a link to the page.
  • The DorkOS account link itself stays in Settings › Access. This tab only shows that it's being used for apps.
