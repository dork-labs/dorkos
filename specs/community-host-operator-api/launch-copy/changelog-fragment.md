### Added

- If this DorkOS is linked to a DorkOS account, the community switcher can now **Start a community** for you, or **Move a community here** from another host using its owner export. The app walks you through making the community yours in your browser and connecting this DorkOS to it. A move shows its progress, survives closing the app, and explains any failure in one sentence with what to do next.
- A **Hosted communities** list shows each community your account hosts, including any that are on hold, why, and how to reopen them.
