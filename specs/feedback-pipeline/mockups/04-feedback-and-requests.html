Which name? (click one — "Other" lets you type your own)

A

Sent to the team

Warm, accurate for all three kinds (bug, idea, feedback). Reads as "things I passed to the humans behind DorkOS."

B

Your requests

Short, product-y. Slight tension: a bug isn't quite a "request," but it covers ideas + feedback well.

C

Feedback & requests

Literal and unambiguous. A touch longer, but nobody wonders what's inside.

D

Outbox

One word, a familiar metaphor — the things you've sent out. Distinctive, though it leans email.
