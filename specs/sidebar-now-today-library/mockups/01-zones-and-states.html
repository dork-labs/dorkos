2 · How the Library actually works

Your nesting worry is solved by one rule: zone labels are landmarks, sections are accordions. "Library" (like "Now" and "Today") never collapses — it's a heading, not a control. Only the sections inside it fold.

The rules, stated once
  • Now / Today / Library never collapse. They're landmarks. They appear and disappear based on whether they have content.
  • Channels / DMs / Agents are the accordions. Click anywhere on the row to toggle. No nested accordions below except agent groups (one level, inside Agents).
  • Selecting vs expanding is never ambiguous because section rows only expand — real destinations (a channel, an agent, a session) are always leaf rows.
  • Collapsed ≠ silent. Badges and working-counts roll up.
  • Small fleets default to expanded; sections you fold stay folded.
  • Alt-click any chevron: collapse/expand everything (Slack/Linear convention).

One door for every "make a thing"Your issue 7 dies here: this menu is the only create surface. The hover ＋ on each section is just a shortcut into the same menu, pre-selected. ⌘N and Enter give the fast path: a new session with your last-used agent, instantly.
Ask DorkBot — help that answers backThe ✦ Ask DorkBot button lives permanently in the footer strip (and as the first Getting-started suggestion). Clicking it doesn't open docs — it opens a fresh DorkBot session pre-seeded with context: what page you were on, your fleet size, recent errors from the session you were viewing. DorkBot greets you already knowing the situation ("Looks like your Codex runtime disconnected — want me to walk through reconnecting?"). A help menu item can't do that; an agent can. It's also the product's own thesis — the system explains itself through an agent.
Implementation noteSeeding = a hidden context preamble on session start (server-side), not visible chat text. DorkBot already exists as the auto-created system agent, so this is wiring, not new machinery.

  • The drawer dies. No hamburger-sidebar on mobile. Now + Today become the Home tab — the sidebar's brain, promoted to the whole screen.
  • Four tabs: Home (badged with needs-you count) · Library · DorkBot · You. Slack cut to 3 tabs; Discord runs 3. We spend our fourth on help-that-answers.
  • No FAB (nobody in this category uses one) — "New" stays in the header.
  • Hover doesn't exist: row actions move to long-press (context menu already exists and is identical to ⋯ by construction — the dual-render menu system pays off here). Rows grow to 40–44px touch height.
  • Bulk first: a "Catch up" action at the top of Today marks everything read — per-item triage is a desktop behavior.
  • Approvals are the killer mobile feature: permission prompts land in Now with approve/deny right there. Your agents keep moving while you're in line for coffee.

5 · The moments

The welcome-back glowAn agent finished while you were away → when you return, its row glows amber once and fades (you can see it looping in the "Quiet morning" mock). Not a badge, not a toast — a warm "this happened for you."
The all-clearWhen you handle the last needs-you item, the Now zone doesn't just vanish — it settles: a brief "All clear ✓" beat, then the zone folds away and Today rises. Finishing your queue should feel like finishing.
The morning digestFirst open of the day: one sunrise row atop Today — "While you were away: 2 finished, #team +5." Click it for the full story; ignore it and it dissolves. The product greets you exactly once.
Suggestions that retireGetting-started items check themselves off and disappear forever when done. The grouping hint appears only at 8+ agents. Chrome earns its place by being temporary.
The approve-from-anywhereA permission prompt slides the Now zone in wherever you are — desktop or phone — with the approve action inline. The moment users realize they can unblock an agent from the couch is the moment DorkOS becomes indispensable.
