The current conversation is always Today's first rowHere you're chatting with Mio Clicker PM — an agent that would be 40 rows deep in a big Library. Doesn't matter: the moment you open any conversation, it anchors to the top of Today with its live status on the row (working pulse + activity verb, or idle, or needs-you).
  • No new section needed. Today already exists and sits directly under Now; its first row is always visible without scrolling (Now is height-capped at 3 + one working line).
  • Why not the Now zone? Now means "needs you." Your open conversation doesn't need you — you're already there. Putting it in Now would teach users to ignore Now. It only enters Now if it genuinely blocks (a permission prompt), like anything else.
  • The anchor is a status instrument: the active row carries the working verb, so the thing you said you use the sidebar for — "is my agent doing something?" — is answered at a fixed position, always. A fixed position beats a found position.
  • When you close or switch conversations, the row un-pins and falls back into natural recency order.

Origin marks — one vocabulary everywhere
🐙You ↔ agent — no mark Task / scheduled Telegram / external chat Room-triggered Agent-to-agent
  • The mark is a small muted glyph in the trailing slot — it says how this session started, never competing with unread/working signals.
  • Same glyphs in Today rows, the switcher, "+N automated", and Activity. Learn once, read everywhere.
  • Automated sessions never claim a top-level Today row. They stay behind "+N automated" — unless one needs you, and then it's in Now anyway. Human attention is the scarce resource; automation doesn't get to spend it by default.

Suggestions are generated, not scriptedYou're right to distrust "Add your first agent." With onboarding scanning the disk and seeding a starter team, the honest first step is "Meet the agents we found" — review and confirm, not create.
  • Each slot is computed from real state: discovery found agents → "Meet the N agents we found." Found none → "Add your first agent" (the fallback world). Agents exist but never ran → "Start your first session." Never a static checklist.
  • This is the same engine as Now. Getting-started items are just needs-you items for a brand-new account. One system, one zone, two life stages — the zone literally becomes Now as real signals take over.
  • Items retire permanently when done, and the zone disappears after all three — the user graduates to the calm sidebar.
