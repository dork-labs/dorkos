## Found and fixed along the way

- **#2342:** the session-access browser test grew past its 30 s budget after H10 (six reloads) and failed other people's queue builds. It is split into three tests of at most one reload each.
- **Queue-only breaks from copy:** H6 renamed way display names, and a notifications spec found its route by the old name (fixed to look up by type). H7 made a Read choice report `level: 'read'`, and an owner-management spec expected none.

## Lessons

- Display names and copy are not identifiers. Tests and code that match on them break when the words improve.
- Five parallel branches each added a migration. Every merge renumbered the next one; regenerate with drizzle-kit, never by hand.
- `watch-prs.sh` started as a background task only reports on exit, so ejections sat unseen. Filed as DOR-2558.
