**Words.** A **team space** is a space with more than one person in it, on a space server. A **home** is a DorkOS that holds an agent's space side: a person's own computer, or their DorkOS in the cloud (the **cloud home**). A **teammate** is a member of a team space who is not the owner of the agent in question. **The freeze** is rule 4 of `AGENTS.md`, read as item 17's spec reads it (its M10): nothing under `services/spaces`, `apps/space` or `services/spaces/migrate.ts`, nothing in the community wire (`packages/shared/src/community-wire*`), and nothing in `packages/shared/src/community-adapter.ts` or the shared enums it reuses (`RoomKindSchema` and `AuthorKindSchema`, `packages/shared/src/room-schemas.ts:46`, `:51`) changes until the Community Space has opened and its opening checks are green. This spec also treats the space's own page, `apps/client/src/space`, as frozen: `apps/client/vite.space.config.ts:8-20` builds it into `apps/space/dist` (§8.8 proposes the sentence for `AGENTS.md`).

## The short version

- **Three of eight are built.** DMs and mentions with a member's agent (C6), the teammate rules (C7) and the space page protections (C8) are done. C7 was built in a changed form: a teammate's turn runs at the agent's own level, with a teammate mark, a monthly limit for each space, and a card for big actions.
- **Two belong to item 13 now.** Signing in anywhere (C11) and the self-hosted wake (C12) were re-based by item 13's spec, which carries them. Nothing of them is left here.
- **Three are mostly to build.** Creating and inviting (C5), the wake for a mention (C9) and seats (C10). Each splits into a part that can start now, a part that starts once its contract step is answered, and a part inside the freeze.
- **The invite link stays the space page's.** The app can make an invite and share today's link now; the link opening the app at "Join this space" waits for the freeze.
- **A member with no computer needs little on top.** Item 13 gives them their DorkOS in the cloud; item 14 gives their agents a cloud computer. A team space adds: the cloud home joins a space and adds its agents as members, it wakes when one of its agents is mentioned, and the teammate rules apply there as on a Mac.
- **A space never wakes a Mac.** The space asks the hosted service to wake a sleeping cloud home. A Mac that is off is shown as away, and the mention waits in the space until it comes back.
- **An agent joins a team space as itself.** "Start a team space from this" adds agents as members. It never copies an agent; each agent keeps its one home.
- **Who a teammate is judged by.** In the team space, by the space's own role rules until item 12's team-space directory lands, inside the freeze. On the agent's home, the member tested is the agent, bounded by its owner, and the marks and the card hold the rest.
- **Everything the hosted service does is asked for through `packages/cloud-api`, contract first.** Three contract changes are named (§6).

## Goals

- A person creates a team space from the app, on DorkOS Cloud or on their own server, and invites by link; nobody types an address.
- A member with no computer takes part fully: their agents in the cloud join, reply and wake.
- Teammates reach your agents only through the space, and your big actions come to you as a card.
- Every step outside the freeze ships alone and leaves the app working.

## Non-goals (out of scope)

- The design of the space server's half, inside the freeze (§5.4 names it).
- The values of the levels below Owner (equal accounts, ADR `261006-235239`).
- Two homes working together (item 13's §7.5), moving a home, or moving an agent between homes.
- A team space's doc as a published page. Item 19's stage 1 does not build it (its §14.3), and it stays later here too.
- A team space whose home is a Mac (item 16).
- Prices, plans and anything else about the hosted service beyond its public contract. As the roadmap says: people outside the US can join; paying stays US-only. Team billing is seats only.

### 1.2 The teammate rules as built

- **The level.** A teammate's turn runs at the agent's set level, like any other (`services/rooms/limits/teammate-turn.ts:5-7`, DOR-3440). The parent's "stranger tier" is not what was built.
- **From off this machine.** A teammate's mention reaches the agent as a turn from somebody off this machine, and never steers the owner's running turn (`services/communities/remote/__tests__/space-stranger-safety.test.ts:122`, `:210`). The turn gets the folders the room's placement granted, as any other (`services/rooms/__tests__/room-turn-runner.test.ts:1229`).
- **The teammate mark.** Every turn a teammate's words start, and every hop after it, carries the mark (`services/core/turn-power/teammate-mark.ts:1-12`). It counts against the space's monthly limit and is refused when the limit is reached (`teammate-mark.ts:17-18`).
- **The outsider mark.** Turns from anyone but the owner in a room carry it, on by default (`env.ts:186-190`, DOR-3858). In a marked turn, standing changes and acts in an outside account come to a person as a card every time (`services/core/capabilities/marked-turn-holds.ts:31-46`).
- **The card.** It shows the action, a summary and the arguments, who asked, an expiry, and whether "Always allow" is offered (`packages/shared/src/approval-schemas.ts:563`, `:591`, `:633`); the rule for "Always allow" is `services/core/approvals/always-offered.ts:39`.
- **Rate limits.** At most three wakes per person and twenty per channel every ten minutes (`services/communities/remote/wake/teammate-wakes.ts:5-8`; `answer-in.ts:31`).
- **The turn's tier.** `owner` or `teammate`, the only trust fact a turn start carries over the wire (`packages/shared/src/session-relay-agent-computer.ts:256`).
- **The space's own roles.** The space server decides what a member may do there with `requireLiveRole` (`services/spaces/data.ts:571`), frozen.

## 4. What a team space needs on top of items 12, 13 and 14

### 4.1 Who a teammate is judged by (item 12)

Item 12 asks one question for every action: may this member do this in this space (`mayMember`, its §4). Its decided rows allow only a member at Owner; the levels below are placeholders that refuse everything until equal accounts give them values (its §4.3). For a team space this means:

- **In the team space itself**, what a teammate may do (post, invite, remove) is the space server's own role rule (`requireLiveRole`, `services/spaces/data.ts:571`); only owners and admins invite (`invites.ts:165`). Item 12's G13 shares the level type with it after the opening. A team-space member directory for the predicate (item 12's §5.3, its last row) is F4, inside the freeze, and it needs the values for the levels below Owner first, signed by Security.
- **On the agent's home**, a teammate's turn is the agent's turn. The member tested is the agent, bounded by its owner's level (item 12's M6). The turn's tier (`teammate`), the marks and the placement's folders hold the rest. This confirms the pick of item 12's open question 7 (§10, question 1).
- **The parent's "stranger tier plus ask the owner"** (P5) maps to: the agent's own level, the teammate mark, the outsider mark, and the card a marked turn raises. Built, except T5's two card rules.

### 4.2 A member with no computer (items 13 and 14)

Item 13 gives the person their DorkOS in the cloud as the home of their private space; item 14 gives their agents a cloud computer that starts when messaged and stops when idle. On top of those, a team space needs exactly four things:

1. **The cloud home joins a team space and adds its agents as members**, as a Mac does today, from the web. The code is the same; T10 proves it on the cloud home.
2. **The cloud home wakes for a mention** of one of its agents (C9's new text). T2 and T8 outside the freeze, F3 inside it. When the home is awake, a teammate's post starts a run, and item 14's starter (its Z8) starts the computer if it is stopped.
3. **The teammate rules and the monthly limit apply on the cloud home** as on a Mac. The cost of a teammate's turn there is the owner's, within the space's limit. T10 proves it.
4. **Each cloud computer is its owner's** (item 14's H7). A team space links no computer. Item 8's column for "a computer someone else linked" (its §5.3) stays unused by item 15.

Nothing else: sign-in on the web, the list of homes, the inbox and search are item 13's; starting, stopping and paying for a computer are item 14's.

### 4.3 Waking (items 9 and 13)

- **Item 9's wake wakes a chat inside a home.** The space's wake wakes a home. They meet at the turn port: once the home is awake, the mention reaches the agent as a room-style run (item 17's M5), not through item 9's registrations.
- **Item 13's cloud home wake** (its §5.4) reports schedules and wakes the home when its owner opens the web or a message is sent to an agent there (its K2). The space's wake (T2) is a sibling of K2: the same front door, with a token the space holds, scoped to one cloud home and one space, and capped as the cloud home's own next wake is.
- **C9's "a wake token for each paired computer"** is replaced by a token for each cloud home whose wake setting is `members` (N6). A space never wakes a Mac. Item 13's C12, a self-hosted way in that wakes its owner's Mac, is item 13's and is not a space's wake.
- **C12's "the self-hosted hub's own wake channel"** is replaced by item 13's C12. A self-hosted team space has no space wake.

### 4.4 Reaching a teammate's agent (items 10 and 17)

A teammate reaches an agent only by posting in the space (N3). Item 17 makes the space's conversation the log and starts the run through the turn port; a remote space's own record stays the space's, frozen (item 17's M10). Item 10's `agent.send` adds no door for a teammate: an agent is closed by default to everyone but its owner and the owner's programs (item 10's A8).

### 4.5 Seats

Seats only. The contract has organizations, members, invitations and seats (§1.3). It lacks a team kind on the start request (T1), the space's wake (T2) and a seat preview for one invite (T3). The app shows the hosted service's words as given, as `SeatManagement.tsx` does.
