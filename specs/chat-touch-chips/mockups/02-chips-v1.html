Option A

Wrap & fold

Chips wrap naturally up to one row while live; everything past that folds behind a "+N more" chip. Click to expand the full set inline (demo auto-cycles the expand). Calmest option — the transcript never gets taller than you asked it to be.

Option B

Flow ticker → grouped summary

While live: one row, newest chip slides in from the right, older ones drift left and fade out — a conveyor of what the agent is touching right now. When the turn settles, the ticker collapses into grouped totals that expand on click. Most "alive"; the history is summarized, not sprawled.

Option C

Kind decks — stacked piles that fan out

Chips cluster into three overlapping stacks — reads, edits, URLs — each with a running count badge. New chips shuffle onto the top of their pile. Hover a deck in the demo to fan it out. Compact and a little playful; the piles physically grow as the agent works.
