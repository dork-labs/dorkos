Two calls I made — tell me if either is wrong

1

“managed by you” is hidden, not shown

Every agent is yours today, so printing it on every row is zero information and pure noise. The line only appears when the owner isn't you — “managed by Priya”. Absence is the signal, the same way people get no bot glyph. Click if you'd rather always show it.

2

Rename and Archive keep their existing entry points

I said B retires three surfaces. On inspection it cleanly retires one: RoomTopicDialog. Inline rename on the sidebar row is a good gesture that matches group rename, and the archive confirm is the right pattern — so the sheet adds a second door to each rather than replacing them. Click if you want them removed from the row menu entirely.
