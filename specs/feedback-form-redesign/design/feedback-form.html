Feedback, simplified

Four things: a smaller menu, an email we can always reply to, words required, and a friendlier form. Click the one you like in each section. Tell me in the terminal what to change.

1 · The help menu

Five choices plus a sub-menu become three. "Send feedback" and "Report a bug" were the same form, so they merge. GitHub moves inside the form as a small link, so nothing is lost.

2 · The form

Attachments are always visible, but small. They sit inside the message box, like a chat composer, so the form reads as "one box, then Send". Diagnostics become two tiny switches. The email box appears only when we can't already reply to you.

4 · Small delights

None of these add clutter. They just make it feel looked-after.

✓
Sent. Thank you!We'll email you@example.com when it's fixed. See your reports

The thank-you says what happens next, and links to your history.

• Draft kept if you close by accident. Reopen and it's all there.
• ⌘↵ sends. Esc closes.
• Paste or drop a picture anywhere on the form (works today, now said once in the 📎 tooltip, not three hint lines).
• Kind switches itself: pointing at something turns it into a Bug (already true).
• Double-send guard: the same report twice in a minute is caught (Ikechi's FB-52 and FB-53 were 33 seconds apart).

Behind the scenes (no UI, just better reports)

• The element is sent as its own field. The Linear title comes from your words, never "Element: [data-testid=…]" (5 of Ikechi's 6 tickets had that as the title).
• Repeated errors in diagnostics are grouped ("× 14") instead of flooding the list.
• Errors that showed up as {} keep their real message.
• The page label "cockpit" in reports is renamed.
