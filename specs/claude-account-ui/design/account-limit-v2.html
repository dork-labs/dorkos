When a session's account runs out: without flow vs with flow

DorkOS always shows the notice, lets you pick another account, or wait. Flow, when installed, adds a recommendation, hides kept-out accounts, keeps Main in reserve, and can move the work automatically. The question for you is only where the notice sits: A or B. Each option shows both states.

A

A banner above the message box

Stays in view where you'd type next, until it's resolved.

A card in the conversation, where it stopped

Reads as part of the story, but scrolls away; the status chip and sidebar dot carry the "out" state after that.
