The Flow panel in DorkOS: your accounts and what's running, in one place

The Flow plugin adds this panel (it's the flow fleet screen, made visual). It shows each account's usage and which task each session is working on, and lets you pause flow or move a task. Where should it live?

A

A "Flow" tab in the right-side panel

It's always one click away next to any chat, like the other right-panel tabs. The top half shows accounts, the bottom half the running tasks.

My pick: A. The point of the panel is glancing at it while you work, so it belongs next to the chat. Clicking a running task opens its session; clicking an account opens its details. Only shown when flow is installed.
