# Everything is a space: the build plan

**Status:** Specified. Planning only; no code lands from this document.
**Author:** Claude, for Dorian Collier
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Linear:** DOR-2932 (umbrella, parent DOR-2735), project "Everything is a space"; one ticket per step below
**Carries out:** ADR [261015-042933](../../decisions/261015-042933-everything-is-a-space-hubs-space-servers-and-agent-computers.md)
**Ideation:** [01-ideation.md](01-ideation.md) (the agreed design and Dorian's decisions; read it first)

## Overview

The design is decided (ideation §2, ADR 261015-042933). This document turns it into ordered, sized steps for **three app chats** starting after the weekend usage reset (Saturday, October 17), so that:

- the hub image boots on DorkOS Cloud by **November 3** (fallback **November 10**);
- the whole path works end to end by about **December 22 to January 5**: sign up on the web, land in your own DorkOS, chat with a cloud agent and with your Mac's agents, and work with a teammate in a team space;
- hardening follows, and launch is **early February 2027** (about February 2 to 9).

**Changed 2026-10-15** (ADR [261015-125850](../../decisions/261015-125850-this-computer-and-other-devices.md), which amends ADR 0320; plan [`plans/2026-10-accounts-and-devices.md`](../../plans/2026-10-accounts-and-devices.md)): the login groundwork lands before launch. Requests are classified as this computer, other device, relayed or hub; this computer is trusted by default and other devices always sign in, with sessions bound to the route that made them. That adds steps F0, F1, F3, F4, F5 and D (below) plus the S1 dead-end fix (DOR-2964) ahead of the chats. Dorian accepted the slip that follows ("Let's go with A for the slip"): about 2 weeks (range 1 to 3), so end to end moves from about December 11 and launch from late January (January 19 to 26) to early February 2027. The Community Space soft launch stays December 8.

DorkOS Cloud builds its half in four chats of its own (the hub fleet and front door, the agent-computer fleet, team spaces, billing). This spec names each place the app waits on Cloud, but Cloud's plan lives in Cloud's own repo. Cloud also owns, with no app step: the front door and warm pool, the redirect to your own DorkOS (only through dorkos.ai, only if you already have one), archiving a free one unused for six months, the model gateway token, the step-up passkey popup, and the Public Suffix List entries.

"Hub" is the internal word. Every string the app renders says **Private** or **space** instead (VOICE).

## Goals

- Experience 1: at dorkos.ai you see and use every agent you have, on your Mac and in the cloud; a Mac that is off shows its agents away.
- Experience 2: a person who never installs anything signs up, pays, and runs agents on a cloud agent computer.
- Team spaces on Cloud at launch, with the full app for a teammate who has no computer.
- The private space is locked by structure and reachable by its owner from anywhere.
- Every DorkOS you sign in to lists the spaces you belong to, so you never type a space's address (P7).
- Everything the app needs is in the public repo: the hub role, the relay protocol, the space server. A self-hoster runs all of it with no DorkOS account.

## Non-goals (before launch)

- Access levels and guests at invite time (DOR-2743, after launch).
- Claude Code and Codex on cloud agent computers (OpenCode at launch; Doe if the spike says yes).
- Syncing a Mac hub with a cloud hub.
- A Mac as a team space's home (PGlite; server-merge step 9).
- Shared mini apps in spaces, and showing another computer's mini apps (after launch, on a sandbox domain).
- A sandboxed teammate tier that acts directly.
- A self-hosted hub for several people (needs equal accounts).
- Team billing beyond seats.

## The step list

Sizes: **S** under a day, **M** one to three days, **L** three to six days, each for one implementation chat including review. "Depends on" means lands after: work may start earlier against a merged contract, but the step merges only after its dependencies. Every step is one ticket and ends in at least one PR. "Cloud:" names what the step waits on from DorkOS Cloud.

## Testing strategy

- **Contract (A1):** request and response tests in `packages/cloud-api`; a deliberate change updates them in its own PR.
- **Hub mode (A2, A3, A5):** a pooled hub refuses every sign-in; setup routes refuse without a recent owner sign-in; runtimes refuse to start; traffic without the front-door proof is refused; the wrong host is refused; cross-origin and `same-site` requests are refused on changes, WebSocket upgrades and streams. The image boots in CI with provisioning settings.
- **The private space (A6):** a refusal test for each lock (invite, join, share, a second member, a pairing from another account's computer).
- **The relay (B1 to B4):** conformance tests for the protocol on both sides; the relayed caller never passes an "at this computer" or person check; a Mac with login off refuses; replayed and forged approvals fail; the version handshake shows "needs an update" both ways; revoke works with the network off.
- **Teammates (C7):** a teammate turn with the stranger tier refuses shell and connections; "ask the owner" runs exactly the approved arguments, refuses "allow always", expires, and rate-limits.
- **Space pages (C8):** response headers pinned per route.
- **End to end (L1a):** browser tests for both experiences and the teammate path, against Cloud's test environment.
