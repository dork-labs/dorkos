## Profile

- **Company size**: one founder, or a founding team of two to five people, plus many agents
- **Stage**: pre-seed to seed, or bootstrapped. Often accelerator-shaped (think YC): ambitious, moving fast, short on people
- **Industry**: any. Software is common, but the business might be a service, a store, an agency or a media company

## Characteristics

- The founder is semi-technical: sets up tools, reads what an agent did, and decides; does not need to write code
- Agents do most of the work across functions: building the product, support, sales follow-up, operations, finance chores, content
- Runs on ordinary business tools (Gmail or Google Workspace, a calendar, payments, accounting) and wants agents to work in them
- Already pays for at least one AI model subscription or API account
- Wants to own the system: comfortable running it on their own computer, and open to an optional hosted layer when it saves real effort
- Talks to a few people (cofounders, contractors, advisors) and many agents, and wants them in one place

## Why They Adopt DorkOS

They have outgrown a chat box and a pile of disconnected tools. A workspace where people and agents talk in DMs, channels and threads is the given; plenty of products will offer that. They choose DorkOS for three things, in this order: **mini apps** (they ask for a tool the business needs, and their agents build it inside DorkOS), a product **built for founders** rather than for everyone, and **ownership** (their computer, their files, their own AI plan, open source and free). They also want agents to act in the outside tools the business runs on, as trusted colleagues with a record anyone can check, not suspects that need approval for every step.

## Why They Don't Adopt

- They use AI only occasionally, as a writing helper
- They want someone else to run everything for them (the anti-persona, `the-prompt-dabbler.md`)
- Their business lives entirely inside one vendor's suite that already does what they need

## Revenue Signals (future monetization context)

- The local app is free forever. Revenue comes from DorkOS Cloud, which is optional: remote access, credits, and later hosted agent computers, publishing beyond the free limits, and email addresses
- Would pay to keep agents reachable and working while the laptop is closed
- Would not pay for the core open-source app itself
