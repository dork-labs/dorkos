# DorkOS Vision

> **Canon.** Agreed with the founder on 2026-10-06 and 2026-10-07. This file and its three siblings are the north star for everyone who works on DorkOS, people and agents alike: [`VISION.md`](VISION.md) (what and why), [`PRINCIPLES.md`](PRINCIPLES.md) (how we decide), [`VOICE.md`](VOICE.md) (how we talk) and [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) (what is built, what is next, and what we may claim). Where anything else in the repo disagrees with them, they win.

## Vision

Millions of solopreneurs and founders use DorkOS every day to earn billions of dollars and compete with companies far bigger than them.

This is the internal north star. It is not marketing copy.

## Mission

DorkOS is the single interface a founder uses to run their whole business: talk to people and agents, and run the outside tools a business needs (Gmail, QuickBooks and the rest).

## What DorkOS is

**A workspace for people and agents.** The long form is "the workspace where founders run their business with people and agents." It works like Slack: DMs, group DMs, channels and threads, with agents working the outside tools the business runs on.

**The picture to keep in mind: DorkOS is the office, and people and agents are the workers who log in.** An agent's brain (DorkOS's own engine by default, or Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode) plugs into the office, and its computer sits outside it and connects in. The office holds the conversations, the docs, who may do what (access levels are roadmap), and the connections to outside tools. The workers bring the skill. Which brains DorkOS supports is a docs fact, never the headline.

## DorkOS first, ready in under a minute

Decided on 2026-10-07. A new person needs no outside AI subscription. They start on DorkOS itself: a DorkOS account, DorkOS Cloud and DorkOS's own AI, and they are up and running in under one minute. DorkOS's own products and services come first everywhere in the app. Bringing your own Claude, ChatGPT or OpenRouter sign-in is the second option. How this is built is in [`PRINCIPLES.md`](PRINCIPLES.md) and [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).

## The message stack

Decided on 2026-10-07. Every public surface tells the story in this order, top to bottom:

1. **You, Multiplied.** The tagline.
2. **Build and run your business with an agent team.** The page title and the main headline.
3. **Your agents join your team chat, take on real work, and build the custom tools your company runs on.** The supporting line.

How to use these lines (casing, the exact page title, what headlines may never say) is in [`VOICE.md`](VOICE.md).

## What sets us apart

**Table stakes, not our edge: a chat workspace with people and agents.** It is what DorkOS is, and we may say so plainly. Everyone will have one soon, so it is never the reason to choose DorkOS.

**Three things make us different, always in this order:**

1. **Mini apps.** Ask for a tool your business needs, and your agents build it inside DorkOS. This leads every pitch, feature list and launch video.
2. **Built for founders.** Not general purpose. Goals, business connections and ready-made founder mini apps are where it is going.
3. **Ownership.** Your agents, tools, files and data stay yours, wherever they run.

What each of these does today, and what is still coming, is in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).
