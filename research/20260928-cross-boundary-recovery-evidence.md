## Focused Cloud lifecycle regression

At the pinned baseline, `apps/server/src/services/core/auth/cloud-link.ts` accepts a heartbeat response after awaiting it without checking whether its captured credential still owns the current link. A late success can set the state/account label after unlink; a late unauthorized response calls `markUnlinked()` and can clear a newer credential. `withManagedConnectorToken` similarly clears on an old request's unauthorized result. `unlink()` awaits remote revocation before clearing current configuration, so a newer link can also settle in that interval.

Eight initial deferred-response cases failed against the baseline implementation. The lifecycle implementation now checks the captured generation, credential and origin before applying a response, and withdraws locally before awaiting remote revocation. The focused `cloud-link-lifecycle.test.ts` and existing `cloud-link.test.ts` passed 25 tests, including equal-token replacement and current transient retry. This proves the bounded local ordering behavior, not remote revocation: the legacy revoke route mismatch remains recorded in the retirement inventory. DOR-2348 keeps credential lifetime distinct from service identity; no broad recovery suite or CI change was added.
