## Architecture now

Local execution remains in Express with interchangeable runtimes. Browser, phone and desktop share the client/Transport path. Local live streams use WebSockets with SSE compatibility; remote Community participation and its browser use qualified HTTP plus resumable SSE. Spaces is the product name; the independent Community Hono/PostgreSQL/blob service retains content and membership authority. Cloud supplies optional account and hosting management. The public app and independent Community remain runnable without Cloud.

## Next architectural work

1. Select the DOR-2482 laboratory cohort from the shipped design. Prove per-owner permissions before production handoff: unknown, failed or timed-out writers cannot authorize reset, storage closure or replacement startup. Preserve DOR-2483 startup rollback and DOR-2484 retention scopes.
2. Reconcile DOR-2349 for interrupted moves, backup followed by erasure reapplication, installation revocation, replica cleanup and marketplace recovery. Reuse evidence before adding suites.
3. Shape DOR-2346 lock ownership/cancellation independently of response transport. Coordinate future runtime process reuse; avoid a transcript-store or all-Transport rewrite.
4. Finish existing Cloud work: explicit payer choice under DOR-2623, remote enrollment/ingress under DOR-2086, managed migration under DOR-1798 and gated website retirement under DOR-2442.
5. Align project/extension lifetime with recovery enumeration. Seen projects and cross-project orchestration now reach beyond the older Mesh-derived janitor census. Extension-reported roots must not become discovery authority to widen cleanup.

These priorities are reflected in the [roadmap](../plans/architecture-improvement-roadmap.md#disposition-snapshot-2026-10-01). They propose bounded next outcomes, not new readiness or implementation claims.
