## September 28 decision and evidence limit

Do not delete the site's account implementation yet. The prerequisite is a full production release after account handover with no rollback. The public evidence inspected does not establish that prerequisite. Merged code, a configured forwarding origin, historical acceptance, and a production release are different evidence. Record the release/deployment identifiers, handover interval and rollback disposition before making the retirement decision.

This is a source and test inventory with the bounded fresh verification below, not a production probe. No deployment, account change, paid service, or live event was exercised. All paths below are relative to `apps/site/` unless stated otherwise.

## DOR-1798: historical acceptance and today's capability gate

The [implementation record](../specs/white-label-connections/04-implementation.md#current-production-acceptance--september-11) records real managed Accounts acceptance on September 11: OAuth completion, one exact harmless read, the same single attempt in hosted/local usage, and denial after grant revocation. It also records Accounts promotion and later cleanup of temporary keys. Credit that proof; historical unfinished P2 paragraphs later in the record do not erase it.

That evidence does not establish managed availability after account handover. The current public site deliberately disables managed capabilities while forwarding is on. Restoring them requires a separately owned migration and each capability's current acceptance evidence; the Accounts proof is not permission to advertise events or an unverified destination service.

Live event acceptance remains open in the record: one genuine signed event must persist while the local runtime is offline, deliver after restart, clear protected payload on ACK, and finish with exact subscription revocation and upstream disablement/removal. Two previous windows saw no matching event and were cleaned up. A proposed test email was not authorized or sent in that record. Hermetic ingress, retry, ACK and revocation tests remain useful but cannot supply the missing live event.

The bounded next actions are to reconcile current ownership and per-capability migration evidence, preserve the existing fixture boundaries, and obtain the missing production-release receipt. No unowned implementation remainder was established by this source inventory; neither DOR-1798 nor DOR-2442 should close on this document.
