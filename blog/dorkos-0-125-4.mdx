DorkOS 0.125.4 names a signed-out Claude account, and signs in to that account, not your default.

## Highlights

- **The right account gets signed in.** If you use more than one Claude account, the banner now says which one is signed out. Its **Sign in** button signs in to that account, not your default one.

## All Changes

### Fixed

- When a Claude account is signed out, the banner names it, and **Sign in** signs in to that account, not your default.
