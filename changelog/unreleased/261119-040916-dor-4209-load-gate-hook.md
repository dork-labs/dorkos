### Added

- When the computer is busy, heavy jobs that agents on Claude Code run, like builds, test suites and installs, now wait their turn, so the computer stays usable. They run one at a time, first come first served, across every such agent on the computer. A waiting chat shows its place in line, and a job that has waited four hours runs anyway. How busy counts as busy, and how long a job may wait, are both settings you can change. Quick commands like `ls` never wait. A job that needs your OK gives up its turn while it waits for you, and runs as soon as you approve. Agents on Codex and OpenCode do not wait yet. A heavy job an agent sends to the background waits for its turn to start, but does not keep its turn while it runs (DOR-4209)
