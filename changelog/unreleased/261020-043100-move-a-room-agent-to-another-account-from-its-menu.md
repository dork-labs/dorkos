### Added

- Move an agent in a channel or DM to another Claude account yourself: open its menu in the room's member list and choose **Continue on another account…**. Pick an account and its next answer starts there, with a summary of where it was. If no account has usage left, the picker says so.
- Agents now know what it means when their room seat moves to another account, so they finish what they started instead of answering twice.
