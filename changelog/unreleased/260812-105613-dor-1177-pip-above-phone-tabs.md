### Fixed

- On a phone, tucking a pinned panel away used to park it right on top of Home, Library,
  DorkBot and You — the four buttons that get you anywhere in DorkOS. Every one of them was
  unpressable until you closed the panel. The panel now sits just above them, so you can
  keep moving around while something stays pinned, and lists stop above it too instead of
  hiding their last row behind it (DOR-1177)
