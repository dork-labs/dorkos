### Fixed

- Blocking an agent's to-dos now stops it. Before, a Blocked saved for the to-do tools did nothing, and the agent could still make, change and read to-dos. They now have their own **To-dos** area in Permissions, Allowed by default, where you can set Blocked or Ask. A Blocked saved before this update now applies too. (DOR-3936)
