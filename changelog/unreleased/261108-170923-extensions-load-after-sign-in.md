### Fixed

- With login on, your mini apps now show up in the sidebar's Add-ons section, and their tabs show up in Settings, as soon as you sign in. Before, they stayed missing until you reloaded the window.
- When your mini apps can't be loaded, the sidebar and Settings → Extensions now say "Couldn't load apps." with a Try again button. Before, it looked as if you had none installed.
