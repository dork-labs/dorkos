### Added

- Hand a new community to its owner with one link. When a host administrator creates a community, they now get an owner claim link to copy and send. The owner opens it, creates an account or signs in, and lands in their new community as its owner. The link works once, lasts 24 hours, and never travels to the server in the address bar. (DOR-2181)
