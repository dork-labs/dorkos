### Changed

- In the Inbox, an extension that wants to run now says so plainly: "Flow wants to run on this computer." It lists what it can reach on three short lines, Access, Runs as and Network, and you answer with Allow or Not now.
- An extension whose files changed after you allowed it now says its screens are paused until you look. It shows what changed and when, and you answer with Allow the new version or Keep paused.
