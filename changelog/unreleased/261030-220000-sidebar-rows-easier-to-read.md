### Changed

- Show "needs you" in the sidebar as a small orange diamond, not an amber dot. The dot was hard to see on the light theme and looked like the green and grey dots beside it. An agent with live chats keeps the diamond next to its "live" chip.
- Make the sidebar easier to read: the faded line under a row's name, the Find label, an empty section's hint, and the mute and pause icons are darker, and the "you can drop here" ring is easier to see.
- Make sidebar icons and agent faces bigger on a phone, so they fit their row. The "⋮" on a row is a little bigger with a mouse too.
- Show a space's channels and direct messages as normal sidebar rows, with the agent's face on each direct message. A busy channel is bold instead of showing a number, a direct message keeps its count, and the arrow keys move through them like any other section.
