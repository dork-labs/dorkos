### Fixed

- When your agent hands you the Agent browser to sign in, the card now has a **Done signing in**
  button from the moment the sign-in window opens. On a Mac, closing that window left Chrome
  running, so DorkOS never saw it close and your agent waited forever. Done signing in closes the
  window, saves your sign-in and hands the browser back. If the sign-in can't be saved, the card
  says so and offers **Sign in again**. **Stop task** leaves the window open for you, and the browser
  panel offers **Done signing in** whenever one is open, so the browser is never left stuck. The
  panel shows the same card as the chat, with the same words and buttons.
