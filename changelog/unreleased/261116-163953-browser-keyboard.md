### Added

- **The Agent browser knows the usual browser keys.** ⌘L, ⌘R and ⌘⇧C work in the browser and in its page. ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward, and Esc stops a load, from the browser's own bar. Inside the page those keys stay the page's, as in Chrome. Outside the browser, ⌘[ and ⌘] still move between your app tabs. The matching Windows and Linux keys work too. (DOR-4432)
