### Changed

- Messages are easier to read in chats and rooms. Text is a little heavier, with more space between lines, and long paragraphs stop at a comfortable width while code and tables still use the full width.
- On a phone, messages have more room, because the side margins are smaller.
- Pointing at a message in a chat no longer tints it grey. Rooms keep their tint, since their messages have actions.
- Day labels are plain centred text, times read "2:03 PM" instead of "02:03 PM", and there's a little less space between turns.
