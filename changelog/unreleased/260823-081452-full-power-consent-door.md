### Added

- The first time you open DorkOS after this update, it asks one plain question: run at full power, or keep being asked first? Choose full power in one click, or keep being asked — your call, and it sticks. Unlocking turns on the whole set at once — agents act without stopping to ask, they message each other across projects, approvals stay put, and scheduled runs get the same freedom — and opens up your agents to talk to one another. Prefer to stay hands-on? "Keep asking me first" changes nothing, and the question never comes back. There's also a "Customize…" link if you'd rather set the pieces yourself in Settings. If the mesh step doesn't finish, DorkOS tells you plainly and leaves everything else on — nothing is turned on behind your back, and nothing you chose is quietly undone.
