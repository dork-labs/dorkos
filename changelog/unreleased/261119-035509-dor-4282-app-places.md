### Security

- A mini app shows up only in the places its approval card lists. The card now names every one, such as a page, ⌘K commands or dialogs.
- When an app lists a new place, it keeps working where you already allowed it. The new place stays off until you allow it, in the Inbox or in Settings. Apps that already used an unlisted place say so in Settings.
- If an update only adds places, the app keeps running and only the new places wait for your OK. Any other change to its files asks again, as every update does.
- An app you allowed before this keeps its tab and Settings page until its files change. Settings asks you to update it when an update is ready.
- When several apps ask only about new places, the Inbox asks about them in one card. You still answer each app on its own.
- DorkOS puts the app's name on its dialogs and ⌘K commands, so you can always tell they come from the app.
- Apps can't use names that start with DorkOS or match DorkOS's own pages, like Settings or Inbox. Accents and look-alike letters count as the plain letter.
