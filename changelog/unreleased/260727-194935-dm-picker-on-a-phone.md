### Fixed

- Starting a direct message works on a phone. The "+" beside Direct messages used to open a small floating panel pinned to a button the phone sidebar had already covered up. It now opens a full-screen sheet: the search box sits at the top where the keyboard can't hide it, the list of agents has room to scroll, and there's an X to close it. On a tablet or a computer it stays the same compact panel it always was.
