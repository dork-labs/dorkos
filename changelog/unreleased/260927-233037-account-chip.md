### Added

- See which Claude account a chat spends and how much it has left, right in the status bar. With two or more Claude accounts, a small chip shows the account's name and two bars for its 5-hour and weekly limits. It turns amber when the account is close to a limit and red when it runs out, and it says when the account comes back. Click it to see each limit and when it resets (DOR-2387)

### Changed

- Pick the account for a new chat on the account chip instead of the runtime menu. The choice still applies to that chat only and locks once you send the first message (DOR-2387)
