### Added

- Talk about a post or task right inside a mini app. A mini app can show a room thread and let you reply there, under your own name. You choose which rooms it uses in Settings → Extensions, and it only sees what you can see there. Agents and other websites can't reply as you through it. Each reply is in the audit trail with the mini app named. A mini app runs with your access, so only turn on ones you trust. (DOR-3017)
