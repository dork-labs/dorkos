### Fixed

- A room with a lot of agents in it no longer grows the sheet off the top of the screen. It stops at a readable height and the middle scrolls, the same way every other panel in DorkOS works.
- Opening the sheet on a phone no longer pops the keyboard at you. The search box is still the first thing under the heading, and still one tap away — you just get to look at the list first.

### Changed

- Rows in the roster are taller on a touch screen, so a face and two lines of text read as a person rather than as a dot with a caption.
