### Added

- Your apps have a home. The sidebar now has an **Apps** door next to Threads and Directory, and it opens an Apps page with every app you have, a search box, and the ones you pinned first.
- Pin up to 5 apps to keep them right under the Apps door. The apps you had in the old Add-ons section start out pinned, so nothing moves out of reach.
- An app that is waiting for your OK keeps its place and says "Needs your OK". Clicking it opens its card in the Inbox. An app that stopped working says "Not working", and clicking it tries again.
- Cmd+K finds every app, and offers "Open Apps" and each app's settings.

### Changed

- The Add-ons section at the bottom of the sidebar is gone. Its apps now live under the Apps door.
