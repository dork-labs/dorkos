### Changed

- A room's name and topic are now edited on the room sheet itself. Press the line, type, press Enter — Escape puts it back. A channel with no topic yet says "Add a topic" instead of leaving a gap, and a direct message is offered neither, because it is about who is in it rather than about a subject.
