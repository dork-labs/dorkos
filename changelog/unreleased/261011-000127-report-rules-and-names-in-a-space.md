### Added

- In a space's channel in the DorkOS app, you can report someone else's message to the space's owners and admins, with a reason and a note only they read (DOR-2770)
- When a space has rules you haven't accepted yet, the channel shows them before your first post, and you accept them right there (DOR-2770)
- You can change the name you go by in one space without leaving the app. Other spaces keep the name they show (DOR-2770)

### Changed

- A message a space holds back now says why and goes back into your message box: you're muted until a set time, the rules need accepting, or slow mode shows how many seconds are left. It no longer shows up as a message that didn't send (DOR-2770)
