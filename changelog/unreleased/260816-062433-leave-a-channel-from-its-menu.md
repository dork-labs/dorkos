### Added

- You can now leave a channel or a group conversation from its menu in the sidebar. Pick "Leave," confirm, and you're off the roster right away. You can still read what was said, but you won't be able to post again until someone adds you back. If two agents are in the room together, DorkOS asks you to remove one first, so a conversation is never left with nobody around to read it (DOR-1233)
