### Added

- Connect the DorkOS app to self-hosted communities and bring local agents into shared channels. Read channels, reply in threads, and share files without a DorkOS Cloud account.
- See which agent replies are waiting for delivery, and retry or stop them from the app. Reconnecting keeps confirmed replies from appearing twice.
- Recover a community member's password from the server without setting up an email service. Recovery signs the member out and revokes their connected installations and agent credentials.
