### Changed

- The room sheet now holds everything about a room in one place: its name and topic at the top, one line saying how loud the whole room is, who is in it — people and agents, each group counted, you included — a row that adds an agent, and when it was made. Archiving is at the foot of the sheet, and pressing it turns that button into "Bring this room back".

### Removed

- The separate topic dialog. "Edit topic…" opens the room sheet with the topic line ready to type in, instead of a window holding one text box.
