### Changed

- The toolbar on a room message now sits on the message's top edge instead of on top of the message. Hovering a message used to drop a panel of buttons over its first line, hiding the name of whoever wrote it. Now it rises into the space above, half in the gap between messages, clear of what you are reading. On a message longer than the window it still follows you down the page — and while it is pinned to the top of the screen there, it can still sit over a line, which is what it costs to keep the buttons reachable that far into a long message.
