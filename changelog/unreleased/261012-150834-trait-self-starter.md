### Changed

- The top Autonomy level is now called Self-starter instead of Rogue. A Self-starter agent takes the next step in its job before you ask, but still waits for your yes on things that need one
- The two top Chaos levels are now called Bold and Daredevil instead of Reckless and YOLO. Both still move fast on their own work, but neither skips the checks that protect your data or does anything it can't undo
- No trait level can make an agent skip asking first anymore. A lower Autonomy level can make it check in more, and that's all a slider changes about asking. Your saved slider positions stay the same
