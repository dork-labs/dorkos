### Added

- When an agent says it kept a promise it made to you, your Inbox tells you, and the promise asks you to confirm it: Yes, or Not yet. Not yet reopens it and wakes the agent to finish it. The agent can link what it delivered, like a pull request. If you don't answer within a day, it shows "Kept · unconfirmed" and asks nothing more.
