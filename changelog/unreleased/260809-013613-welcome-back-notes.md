### Added

- Your agents can leave you a short note in your team channel when you come back after being
  away, so you find out what happened while you were gone without going looking. It is on by
  default, capped at three notes per return, and only counts as a return after four hours away.
  There is a switch for it in Settings → Preferences, and it follows you to every device you
  open DorkOS on. If you want to change how long an absence has to be or how many notes one
  return may produce, both are settings in your config file:
  `dorkos config set welcomeBack.absenceThresholdMinutes 720`.
