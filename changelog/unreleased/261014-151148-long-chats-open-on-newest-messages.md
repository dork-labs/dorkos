### Changed

- A long chat now opens on its newest 150 messages, so it shows up fast however long it is. Press **Earlier messages** at the top to read older ones, 100 at a time. A link to an older message reads back until it finds it, as far as about 2,000 messages back. The "New messages" line still lands in the right place.
