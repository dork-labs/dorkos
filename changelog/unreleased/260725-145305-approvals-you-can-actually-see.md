### Added

- See when an agent is waiting on you, from any screen. A marker in the top bar says how many requests need your approval, and clicking it opens them right there so you can answer without leaving what you were doing.

### Changed

- Approval requests now last two hours instead of ten minutes, so an agent that asks while you are away from your desk does not give up before you get back.

### Fixed

- A request that runs out of time now disappears on its own. It used to sit on screen looking like you could still answer it.
