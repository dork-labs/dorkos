### Security

- When an agent asks to change your settings, the approval card now shows each one as old → new. A list says what it adds and removes, by name. A project's accounts show by their names. A secret says only whether it is set, changed or cleared. Each setting only you may change is marked "needs you", and the card's first line says how many there are (DOR-4775)
