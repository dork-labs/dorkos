### Changed

- Menus, pop-ups, tooltips, dialogs and phone sheets now look and behave the same across the app.
- Menus have even rows with no lines between groups, and they're easier to tap on a phone.
- Tooltips wait a moment before showing, so they no longer flicker as you move past buttons. They don't show on touch screens, and they're no longer a bright white chip in dark mode.
- Phone sheets no longer look like a hole in dark mode. They have a real grabber and left-aligned titles.
- Dialogs open on their first field, and never on a Delete or Close button.
