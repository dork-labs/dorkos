### Added

- The Schedules tab shows when a schedule waits for your OK, failed its last run, or is running (DOR-2820)
- The Activity tab counts what happened since you last opened it, and the Connections tab counts requests waiting for your OK (DOR-2820)
- When the window is in the background, its title count now includes schedules that need a look, beside unread channels (DOR-2820)
- Extensions can put a status, a count and a short note on the tab of their own page (DOR-2820)
