### Added

- Point at the part that looks wrong. In the feedback dialog, "Point at element" gets the dialog out of the way and hands you a crosshair: the app dims, whatever you hover lights up, and one click sends a report cropped to just that piece — with the name we use for it in the code, so we know exactly which one you meant. Press Esc, right-click, or Cancel to back out; anything you had already typed is still there. Desktop and the desktop app only, since it needs a mouse (DOR-911)
