### Changed

- Spaces are now an experiment, and they are off unless you turn them on. Open Settings, then Experiments, and switch on Spaces to join, start or move a space. While it is off, nothing about spaces shows in the app, and your space connections stay saved for when you turn it back on. Your own channels and #team are not spaces and work the same either way (DOR-2740)
- `dorkos community deploy` now says it is experimental before it starts (DOR-2740)
