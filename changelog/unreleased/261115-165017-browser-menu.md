### Changed

- The Agent browser has a ⋯ menu at the end of its controls. It lists the chat's pages, copies the address, opens the page in your own browser, forgets saved sign-ins and closes the browser. On a phone it opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- The tab list, the Disconnect button and the Saved sign-ins row no longer sit above the page, so the page has more room. Disconnect is now called Close browser.
