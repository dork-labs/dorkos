### Added

- An agent's profile now has **What {agent} knows**. It lists what the agent can do in DorkOS, one area at a time, with each area's level right now. Change a level on the Permissions page and the list follows. (DOR-4115)
- **Ask {agent}** on that page starts a new chat that asks your agent what it thinks it can do before it looks anything up, then has it check its real levels and name anything it got wrong. (DOR-4115)
