### Added

- When you answer an agent's permission request, the card settles into the conversation as a receipt — a permanent one-line record of every ask and answer, right where it happened. Allowed and denied requests say so by name; a request nobody answered says it expired and how long it waited. Answering several at once leaves one line with the details a click away.
