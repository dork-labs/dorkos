### Added

- In a room's sheet, pointing at one of an agent's loudness settings — with the mouse or with the arrow keys — now shows what the whole room would become if you chose it. The room's meter and its sentence move to the new answer and are tinted to say they are a "what if". Stop pointing, and they go back. Nothing is saved until you actually pick one.
- An archived room shows no such preview: nobody is triggered there, so there would be nothing true to show.
