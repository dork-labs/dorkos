### Added

- **Remote access** now has one switch at the top of **Settings → Remote access**. Turn it off and only this computer can reach DorkOS: your phone, the tunnel, Wi-Fi and other devices are turned away on their next request. It stays off after a restart. Only you can turn it back on, from this computer. Agents can turn it off. (DOR-3650)
