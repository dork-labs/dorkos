### Added

- **Remote access** now has one switch at the top of **Settings → Remote access**. Turn it off and only this computer can reach DorkOS: your phone and other devices are disconnected at once, whether they came through the tunnel, Wi-Fi or chat from the web. Telegram and Slack keep reaching your agents. It stays off after a restart. Only you can turn it back on, from this computer. Agents can turn it off. (DOR-3650)
