### Changed

- Signed in on your phone or laptop over remote access or any `https` link, you can now do everything you can at your desk: change remote access, the password, trusted package sources and the session relay, and sign your other devices out. Plain Wi-Fi keeps its lower level for now, because anyone on the same network could copy that sign-in.
- Signing a device out now also stops any chat it started and drops what it had waiting to send.
- A sign-in on another device now ends after 90 days, however often you use it. Sign-ins at your desk are unchanged.

### Security

- Too many wrong passwords for one account now pause sign-ins to it for a few minutes, whichever addresses they come from.
- The sign-in cookie can no longer be sent by another website, even through a link.
