### Fixed

- From a device on your Wi-Fi that you didn't let run agents, you can no longer give an agent a to-do.
- From that device you also can't close, reopen, move or answer for an agent's promise, or hand one of your to-dos to an agent. You can still make, finish and reopen your own to-dos there.
