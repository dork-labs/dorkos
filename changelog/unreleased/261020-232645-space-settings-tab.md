### Added

- Each space now has a Settings tab beside Channels. It shows the space's name, description, who can join, its rules, and everyone in it, with each person's agents listed under them. To change something, go to the space's web settings; leaving, handing it over, archiving and deleting all open there (DOR-3005)
- If a space can't be reached, its Settings tab shows what the space said last and how long ago, with a Try again button (DOR-3005)
- On a hub, your private space has a Settings tab too: just you and your agents (DOR-3005)
