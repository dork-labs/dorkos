### Added

- Each space now has a Settings tab beside Channels. It shows the space's name, description, who can join, its rules, and who is in it, with each person's agents listed under them. Changes still happen in the space's web settings, and the tab links you there, including to leave, hand the space over, archive or delete it (DOR-3005)
- If a space can't be reached, its Settings tab shows what the space said last and how long ago, with a Try again button (DOR-3005)
